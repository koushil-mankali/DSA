# Interview Playbook

> For when you have read the chapters and now need to recall, prioritise and practise under time pressure.

## Why This Section Matters

Knowing every algorithm is not enough in an interview. You have to recognise the pattern in a few minutes, pick the right structure and communicate while you code. This section turns the chapters into a revision tool.

| 4 | [Mock Interview Checklist](./04-mock-interview-checklist.md) | 45-minute shape, edge-case list, follow-up map |

## How to Use This Section

1. Skim the cheatsheet once a week.
2. Follow the four-week plan, or compress it if your interview is sooner.
3. Run at least two timed mocks using the checklist.
4. Use the company index to pick extra practice, remembering the tags are community-reported and may be wrong or out of date.

## Prerequisites

You should be comfortable with: the chapters you plan to be tested on.

You do NOT need to know: every advanced chapter. The plan says what to skip.
